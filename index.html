About Me

Computer Science graduate (BSc, First Class, University of Portsmouth) working as a Software Consultant at Imagic, specialising in image processing, computer vision and robotics, and focused on applying technology to improve everyday life.

I'm a logical, creative problem solver who enjoys working across hardware and software, from microcontrollers and sensor networks to ROS-based robots and VR interfaces.

Across personal, academic and professional projects I've focused on software development, IoT, automation, computer vision, robotics, and 3D graphics & animation, with exposure to industry-leading applications. I'm always looking to develop myself and my peers.

I work with multinational clients and system integrators. Previously at Keyence, I implemented camera systems for in-line inspection, gauging and robot guidance, providing computer vision consulting to companies including JLR, GSK, Dyson, Red Bull and Amazon.
